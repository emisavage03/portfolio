Strudel Projects

(2026)

Strudel is a creative coding language for composing music programmatically in real time.

I am especially interested in finding ways to integrate Strudel with TouchDesigner for audioreactive visuals and interactive control.

In May 2026, I built a website to test DOM controls for Strudel and connected it with a visualizer in TouchDesigner. See documentation video below.

In February 2026, I led a creative coding workshop for ZAP USC to teach the basics of live coding in Strudel.

In January 2026, I composed a few works meditating on the sensory experience of the USC campus.
