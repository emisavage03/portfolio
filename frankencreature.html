Franken-creature

(2026)

Frankencreature is a card-driven board game where scientists compete with their lab to build the best Frankensteined creation.

I was responsible for uniting the visual identity of the game with its story and mechanics.

I designed the logo, cards, playmats, and player directions using illustrations from teammates.
